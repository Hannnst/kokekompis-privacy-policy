Overview

KokeKompis ("the app", "we") is a personal recipe cookbook for Android. This policy explains what the app does with data. In short: it stores your recipes and photos on your own device, and it does not collect anything about you.

No collection of personal data

The app has no accounts, no registration, and no login. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It contains no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking software.

What the app stores

Recipes you create, the cover photos you add, and your in-app preferences (theme, language, categories) are stored locally on your device. If your device's operating system performs Android backup or device-to-device transfer, it may copy this local data, which is the standard Android behaviour.

Network access

The app can download a web page only when you provide a URL (to extract a recipe), and it may load a recipe image from a remote URL contained in that page. These requests go directly to the sites you chose; we do not see or log them, and they are not used to identify you.

Permissions

The app requests internet access for the URL and image loads described above. Photos are added through the system photo picker; the app does not request camera or storage permissions and cannot read your photos without your explicit choice.

Third-party software

The app uses only on-device libraries (e.g. on-device text recognition, local database, offline image loading). None of them transmit your data to the app's developer or to advertising networks.

Children

Because the app collects no personal data from anyone, the general privacy considerations do not apply, and there is nothing to target at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new effective date.
